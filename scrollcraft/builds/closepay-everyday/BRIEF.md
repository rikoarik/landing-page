## Grammar and fingerprint

Grammar: Editorial field journal with an interactive foldout. Navigation is a persistent compact contents bar with real chapter anchors. Hero combines oversized type on paper, a photo plate, and an independent alpha foreground. Sequence alternates a photographed cover, quiet prose, one held interactive foldout, a portrait chapter, a document chapter, and a typographic closing invitation. Ending retains the selected business context. Bans: fullscreen media under all copy, fake operational dashboards, repeated feature cards, custom cursors, video chain, scroll hijacking, multiple pinned chapters.

Why the eight stock grammars were rejected: filmic one-shot overweights media; editorial chapters bans the requested visual hero; live surface misrepresents the marketing site as a product; continuous world conflicts with the requested chapters; typographic poster excludes the human photographic world; gallery/catalog lacks the causal order journey; split stage implies a comparison that is not the offer; rhythmic cutlist is too frantic for product comprehension.

Fingerprint registry was empty at planning time. No historical rows to compare.

## Decision reasons

- Palette: original logo's deep petrol, jade accent, and cool paper preserve ClosePay identity; photographic colors are not extra UI accents.
- Type: Archivo's broad heavy forms give a practical commercial-print voice, while Plus Jakarta Sans keeps Indonesian body text easy to read.
- Spacing: a compact cover, quiet prose gap, extended interaction and shorter factual chapters create the requested energy curve.
- Containers: only the physical order slip uses a raised paper treatment to identify an object passing between people; features use open lists.
- Motion: hero depth establishes physical proximity; ticket movement explains a causal workflow; reveal marks a change of business context.
- Fixed theme: paper/editorial print and controlled photographic grading are the authored brand composition; a user theme toggle would alter that art direction.
- Icons: only functional direction and close symbols; no decorative feature icon set.
